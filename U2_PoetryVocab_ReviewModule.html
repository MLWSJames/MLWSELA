The Poem 诗歌原文

April Rain Song By Langston Hughes Let the rain kiss you Let the rain beat upon your head with silver liquid drops Let the rain sing you a lullaby The rain makes still pools on the sidewalk The rain makes running pools in the gutter The rain plays a little sleep song on our roof at night And I love the rain.
"April Rain Song" by Langston Hughes (1921) is in the public domain.

Mood & Atmosphere 情绪与氛围

Remember from your vocab list: atmosphere is the overall feeling a writer creates through word choice and description. In this poem, Langston Hughes uses soft, gentle images — a kiss, a lullaby, a sleep song — to build a calm, peaceful atmosphere around the rain.

回顾你的词汇表：atmosphere（氛围）是作者通过用词和描写营造出的整体感觉。在这首诗中，休斯用温柔的意象——亲吻、摇篮曲、睡眠之歌——营造出一种平静、安宁的氛围。

Tip 提示: Ask yourself — do the words in this poem feel soft or harsh? Warm or cold? That feeling IS the atmosphere/mood.
问问自己——这首诗里的词语给你的感觉是柔和还是刺耳？温暖还是冷淡？那种感觉就是氛围/情绪。

Figurative Language & Sound Devices 修辞手法与声音技巧

Two important devices appear in this poem:

Device 手法Definition 定义Example from the Poem 诗中例子
Personification
拟人		 Giving human qualities or actions to something non-human.
把人类的特质或动作赋予非人类事物。		 "Let the rain sing you a lullaby" — rain cannot really sing, but the poet gives it this human ability.
Consonance
辅音重复		 The repetition of consonant sounds within or between words (not just at the start).
单词内部或之间重复出现的辅音（不仅限于开头）。		 "The rain plays a little sleep song" — the repeated "s" sound creates a soft, musical effect.
Worked Example 示范分析: Let's analyze "Let the rain sing you a lullaby" step by step.
Step 1 — Identify: The rain is described as doing something only people (or living things) can do: singing.
Step 2 — Name the device: This is personification, because a human action is given to something non-human (rain).
Step 3 — Explain the effect: A lullaby is soft and calming, so this personification reinforces the poem's peaceful, comforting atmosphere.
第一步——识别：雨被描写成做了只有人（或有生命的事物）才能做的动作：唱歌。第二步——说出手法名称：这是拟人手法，因为人类的动作被赋予了非人类事物（雨）。第三步——解释效果：摇篮曲是柔和、令人安心的，因此这种拟人手法强化了诗歌平静、抚慰人心的氛围。

Theme 主题

Remember: theme is the message or insight about life that the poem conveys — not just what happens, but what it means. As you take the quiz, think about what Langston Hughes wants readers to feel and understand about rain.

记住：theme（主题）是诗歌所传达的关于生活的信息或感悟——不仅仅是发生了什么，而是它意味着什么。做题时，想一想休斯想让读者对雨产生怎样的感受和理解。
