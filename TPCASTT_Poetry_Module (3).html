ACTIVITY 1

Text & Topic Introduction: What Is TP-CASTT?

TP-CASTT is a seven-step framework that helps readers move from a first, surface-level read of a poem to a deep understanding of its meaning. Each letter stands for one step in the process.

TP-CASTT 是一个七步框架，帮助读者从对一首诗的初步、表面阅读，逐步走向对其意义的深入理解。每个字母代表这个过程中的一个步骤。

StepEnglish中文
T — TitlePredict meaning from the title before reading.在阅读之前，根据标题预测诗歌的含义。
P — ParaphraseRestate the literal meaning, stanza by stanza.逐节重述诗歌的字面意思。
C — ConnotationExamine diction, imagery, and figurative language.分析措辞、意象和修辞手法。
A — AttitudeIdentify the speaker's feelings toward the subject.找出说话者对主题的感受。
S — ShiftsNotice changes in tone, attitude, or perspective.注意语气、态度或视角的变化。
T — Title (again)Reconsider the title now that you understand the poem.在理解全诗后，重新思考标题的含义。
T — ThemeState the poem's central message about life.说出诗歌关于人生的核心信息。

Step-by-Step: A Closer Look

T
Title

Before reading, ask: what might this title mean? What do I expect?

P
Paraphrase

Put each stanza into your own simple words. If you can't paraphrase it, you don't understand it yet.

C
Connotation

Look for word choice, imagery, and figurative language that carries extra meaning.

A
Attitude

Decide how the speaker feels — hopeful, regretful, calm, anxious?

S
Shifts

Watch for turning points — a change in time, tone, or perspective, often signaled by words like "but" or "yet."

T
Title (Revisited)

Look at the title again. Has its meaning grown or changed?

T
Theme

State the poem's message in a full sentence about life or human nature — not just a single word.

Now, Let's Read the Poem

Here's the full poem. Read it once all the way through before we go any further.

这是这首诗的全文。请先完整读一遍，之后我们再继续。

The Road Not Taken — Robert Frost (1916)
Two roads diverged in a yellow wood,
And sorry I could not travel both
And be one traveler, long I stood
And looked down one as far as I could
To where it bent in the undergrowth;
Then took the other, as just as fair,
And having perhaps the better claim,
Because it was grassy and wanted wear;
Though as for that the passing there
Had worn them really about the same,
And both that morning equally lay
In leaves no step had trodden black.
Oh, I kept the first for another day!
Yet knowing how way leads on to way,
I doubted if I should ever come back.
I shall be telling this with a sigh
Somewhere ages and ages hence:
Two roads diverged in a wood, and I—
I took the one less traveled by,
And that has made all the difference.

Worked Example: Applying TP-CASTT

Now let's apply every TP-CASTT step to the poem together, before you try it yourself.

现在，让我们一起用 TP-CASTT 的每一步来分析这首诗，之后你再自己练习。

T — TitleBefore reading, the title suggests a poem about a path someone chose not to walk — maybe about regret or missed chances.
P — ParaphraseA traveler reaches a fork in the road, picks one path, notices later that both looked about the same, and wonders if he'll ever return to try the other.
C — ConnotationWords like "diverged," "grassy," and "trodden black" suggest freshness, separation, and the passage of time. The yellow wood evokes autumn — change and endings.
A — AttitudeThe speaker's attitude is thoughtful and a little wistful — not angry or triumphant, just quietly reflective about an ordinary decision.
S — ShiftsThe final stanza shifts from describing the moment of choosing to imagining himself, years later, retelling the story "with a sigh."
T — Title (Revisited)"The Road Not Taken" now feels less about a literal road and more about every choice whose alternative we'll never know.
T — ThemeEven small, ordinary choices can quietly shape the entire direction of a life.
Notice · 注意
Frost never says the road he chose was actually better — only that choosing it "made all the difference." The poem is more about the *story we tell* about our choices than about the choice itself.
