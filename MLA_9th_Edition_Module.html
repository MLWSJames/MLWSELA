1. Why do we cite sources?我们为什么要标注引用来源？

When you use another person’s words or ideas, you must tell the reader where they came from. This is called citing (or documenting) a source.

当你使用别人的文字或想法时，必须告诉读者它们来自哪里。这叫做引用标注。

  • Give credit to other people’s work. · 尊重他人的成果。
  • Let the reader check the information. · 让读者能够核实信息。
  • Make your paper stronger (more believable). · 让你的论文更有说服力。
  • Avoid plagiarism. · 避免抄袭。
Plagiarism (抄袭) comes from the Latin word plagiarius, which means “kidnapper.” Plagiarism is using someone else’s language, ideas, or other original material without saying where it came from.
Plagiarism 来自拉丁语 plagiarius，意思是「绑架者」。抄袭就是使用别人的语言、想法或原创材料，却不说明来源。
Common knowledge (常识) does not need a citation. Example: “The Earth goes around the Sun.” But you do cite exact quotes, paraphrases, statistics, and special ideas.
常识不需要标注来源。例如：「地球绕着太阳转。」但是直接引用、改写、统计数字和独特的观点必须标注。

3. In-text citations文内引用

An in-text citation is a short note in parentheses inside your sentence. It points the reader to the full entry on the Works Cited page. MLA uses author + page number. The citation looks the same for a quote, a paraphrase, or a summary.

文内引用是写在句子里括号中的简短标注，指向参考文献表中的完整条目。MLA 用作者 + 页码。无论是直接引用、改写还是概括，格式都一样。

Situation · 情况Example · 示例
Author is not in your sentence
句子里没有作者名		Gatsby keeps hoping for a better future (Fitzgerald 55).
Author is in your sentence — put only the page
句子里有作者名——括号里只写页码		Fitzgerald shows that Gatsby keeps hoping for a better future (55).
Source is online with no page numbers — author only
网络来源没有页码——只写作者		Hitchens explains the history of the novel (Hitchens).
Three punctuation rules · 三条标点规则： (1) No comma between author and page: (Fitzgerald 55). (2) No “p.” or “page.” (3) The sentence period goes after the parentheses.
(1) 作者和页码之间没有逗号。(2) 不写 p. 或 page。(3) 句号放在括号后面。

1. Short quotations短引用

A quotation uses the author’s exact words. Put the words in quotation marks. Start with an introductory phrase. After the closing quotation mark, add the citation, and then the period.

直接引用使用作者的原话，要加引号。先写引导语。右引号之后写文内引用，最后写句号。

Intro phrase“quote”(Author page).
Author NOT in the sentence · 句中没有作者 In The Glass Menagerie, the mother Amanda is a true Southern belle, and she recalls, “Among my callers were some of the most prominent young planters of the Mississippi delta” (Williams 981).
Author IS in the sentence · 句中有作者 In The Glass Menagerie, Williams pens Amanda as a Southern belle, and she recalls, “Among my callers were some of the most prominent young planters of the Mississippi delta” (981).
Short quotation = fewer than four lines of prose, or three lines of poetry or less. 短引用 = 少于四行散文，或不超过三行诗。

2. Block quotations块引用（长引用）

If a quotation is longer than four lines of prose (or more than three lines of poetry), set it apart as a block quotation:

  • End your introduction with a colon (:). · 引导语以冒号结尾。
  • Start the quote on a new line. · 另起一行。
  • Indent the whole quote 1 inch from the left margin. · 整段从左边距缩进 1 英寸。
  • Do not use quotation marks. · 不加引号。
  • Put the citation after the final punctuation of the quote. · 引用标注放在引文最后的标点之后。
Sample block quotation (made-up source) · 示例（虚构来源） Writer Dana Lee explains why daily reading matters:
Students who read for twenty minutes each day slowly build a larger vocabulary. They also learn how good writers organize ideas, and that knowledge helps them in every class they take. Over many months, the habit changes how they write. (Lee 12)
Lee’s idea shows that small habits can lead to big growth.

3. Quoting poems引用诗歌

When you quote two or three lines of a poem inside a sentence, separate the lines with a slash with a space on each side ( / ). For poems, cite the author and line numbers, not pages.

在句子中引用两三行诗时，用前后各有空格的斜线（ / ）分隔诗行。诗歌标注作者和行号，而不是页码。

Example · 示例 The poem begins with strange, playful words: “’Twas brillig, and the slithy toves / Did gyre and gimble in the wabe” (Carroll lines 1-2).
The first time you cite lines, write the word lines. 第一次标注行号时，要写出 lines 这个词。

4. Special cases for the author作者的特殊情况

Case · 情况In-text citation · 文内引用
Two authors · 两位作者(Gerson and Gerson 102)
Three or more authors · 三位或以上作者
只写第一位 + et al.（等人）		(Daniels et al. 73)
Two works by the same author — add a short title in italics · 同一作者的两部作品——加斜体短标题(Williams, The Glass Menagerie 999)
No author — use the title in quotation marks (shorten if long, but keep the first words) · 没有作者——用带引号的标题（太长可缩短，但保留开头的词）(“Why Students Need Sleep”)
Quote found inside another source — use qtd. in · 引文来自另一个来源——用 qtd. inJones says, “…” (qtd. in Smith 37).
Some older examples write a comma before et al.. MLA 9 does not: write (Daniels et al. 73). 有些旧的例子在 et al. 前加逗号。MLA 第九版不加逗号。
Secondary source · 二手来源： You read Smith’s article, and Smith quotes Jones. Give credit to both: name Jones in your sentence, and cite Smith with qtd. in. On the Works Cited page, list Smith, because Smith is what you actually read.
你读的是 Smith 的文章，其中 Smith 引用了 Jones。要同时标注两人：句中写 Jones，括号里用 qtd. in 加 Smith。参考文献表里列 Smith，因为你实际读的是 Smith 的文章。

1. The Works Cited page参考文献表页面

The Works Cited page lists every source you used. Every in-text citation must match an entry here.

  • Start on a new page after your paper. · 在论文后另起一页。
  • Write Works Cited centered at the top (no bold, no italics). · 顶部居中写 Works Cited。
  • Use a hanging indent on every entry: the first line is at the left margin, and all other lines are indented. · 每个条目用悬挂缩进：第一行靠左，其余各行缩进。
  • Alphabetize by the first item (usually the author’s last name, or the title if there is no author). · 按第一项字母顺序排列（通常是作者姓氏；没有作者则按标题）。
  • If an entry starts with The, use the next word to alphabetize. · 如果以 The 开头，按下一个词排序。
  • Keep double spacing. · 保持双倍行距。
Example · 示例
Works Cited
Bronte, Charlotte. Jane Eyre. E.P. Dutton and Co., 1922.
Fitzgerald, F. Scott. The Great Gatsby. Hayes Barton Press, 2007.
Gerson, Sharon, and Steven Gerson. Technical Communication: Process and Product. 7th ed., Prentice Hall, 2012.

2. The nine core elements九个核心要素

Every Works Cited entry is built from the same nine elements, in this order. If an element does not apply to your source, skip it.

每个条目都由这九个要素按固定顺序组成。如果某个要素不适用，就跳过。

#Element · 要素Ends with · 结尾Notes · 说明
1Author · 作者.Last name, First name
2Title of source · 来源标题.Italics (big work) or “quotation marks” (small work)
3Title of container · 载体标题,The larger work that holds the source (italics)
4Contributor · 贡献者,Edited by, Translated by, Directed by…
5Version · 版本,7th ed.
6Number · 编号,vol. 39 no. 3
7Publisher · 出版者,Name only, no city
8Publication date · 出版日期,Give all the date information shown
9Location · 位置.Page numbers (p. 74 / pp. 74-82) or URL / DOI
Pattern · 公式：
Author. Title of source. Title of container, Contributor, version, number, publisher, publication date, location.
1 Author.2 Title.3 Container,4 Contributor,5 Version,6 Number,7 Publisher,8 Date,9 Location.

1. What is a container?什么是「载体」？

Many sources are part of a larger work. The larger work is the container. The container’s title goes in italics, followed by a comma.

很多来源是更大作品的一部分。这个更大的作品就是载体，它的标题用斜体，后面加逗号。

Source (small) · 来源Container (big) · 载体
“The Warmth of Glass & Stone” (article)Our State: Celebrating North Carolina (magazine)
“Making the Vaccine Decision” (web page)CDC (website)
“On Leaping Over the Moon” (poem)The Norton Anthology of English Literature (book)
“Grammar Girl: How to Use ‘Who vs. Whom’” (video)YouTube (website)
If a source stands alone (a whole book), it has no container — skip element 3. 如果来源是独立的（整本书），就没有载体——跳过第 3 项。
Web page vs. website: the web page is the small piece (“The Writing Center”); the website is the container (Caldwell Community College and Technical Institute). 网页是小的部分，网站是载体。

2. Elements 4 to 9 in detail第 4 到第 9 项详解

Element · 要素What to write · 怎么写
4 Contributor · 贡献者Other people who helped: Edited by Mansi Daga, Directed by Andrew Stanton, uploaded by Ragan Communications. If you used the editor as the author, do not repeat. · 如果已把编者放在作者位置，就不要重复。
5 Version · 版本Edition or version: 7th ed.
6 Number · 编号Volume and number, with abbreviations: vol. 39 no. 3
7 Publisher · 出版者The organization that produced the work. Do not list the city. · 不写城市。
8 Date · 日期Day Month Year as shown: 27 Apr. 2016. Long month names are shortened (Apr., Sept.). May, June, and July stay in full. · 较长的月份名要缩写；May、June、July 不缩写。
9 Location · 位置Print: p. 74 or pp. 68-71. Online: URL or DOI. · 纸质：页码；网络：网址或 DOI。
URL tip · 网址提示： MLA 9 asks you to copy the URL without http:// or https://. For a DOI, write it as a link: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11069-008-9299-2. (Some examples in your booklet still show http:// — follow your teacher.)
MLA 9 要求复制网址时不要写 http:// 或 https://。DOI 要写成链接形式。（你的小册子里有些例子仍带 http://——请听老师的。）
No date on a web page? · 网页没有日期？ Add the date you looked at it, at the very end: Accessed 21 June 2016. · 在条目最后加上你访问的日期。
