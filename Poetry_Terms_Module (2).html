Concept Review

Figurative Language

Metaphor compares two unlike things directly (no "like/as"). Simile compares using "like" or "as." Personification gives human qualities to something nonhuman. Hyperbole is exaggeration for effect. Metonymy replaces a thing with something closely associated (the "crown" for a monarchy). Synecdoche uses a part to represent the whole ("all hands on deck").

Sound Devices

TermDefinition
AlliterationRepeated initial consonant sounds ("blooming bluebells")
AssonanceRepeated vowel sounds
ConsonanceRepeated consonant sounds anywhere in nearby words
OnomatopoeiaA word that imitates a sound ("Crash!")
Euphony / CacophonyPleasant-sounding vs. harsh-sounding combinations
Internal rhymeRhyme occurring within a single line

Structure Terms

Stanza = a group of lines. Refrain = a repeated line/group of lines. Chorus = a repeated section after each verse. Meter = the pattern of stressed/unstressed beats. Rhythm = the rhythmic pattern of a line. Iambic pentameter = a line of five iambs. Free verse = no regular rhyme or meter. Blank verse = unrhymed iambic pentameter.
