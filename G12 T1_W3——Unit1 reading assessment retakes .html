A. Fix Four Common Sentence Errors · 改正四种常见句子错误

Error · 错误How to spot it · 如何发现How to fix it · 如何改正Example · 例子
Comma splice
逗号拼接		 Two complete sentences joined by ONLY a comma.
两个完整句子只用逗号连接。		 Add a joining word after the comma (and, but, so) OR use a period.
逗号后加连接词（and, but, so）或改用句号。		 ✗ Leo studied hard, he passed the test.
✓ Leo studied hard, so he passed the test.
Run-on sentence
连写句		 Two complete sentences with NO punctuation between them.
两个完整句子之间没有任何标点。		 Add a period, OR a comma + joining word.
加句号，或逗号加连接词。		 ✗ Mina packed her bag she ran to the bus.
✓ Mina packed her bag, and she ran to the bus.
Word form
词形		 A noun is used where an adjective is needed (often after was, felt, very).
需要形容词的地方用了名词（常在was, felt, very后）。		 Change the word to the correct form (see list below).
改成正确形式（见下表）。		 ✗ Leo felt very nerve before his speech.
✓ Leo felt very nervous before his speech.
Verb tense
动词时态		 The verb does not match the time of the story, or does not match the subject (he/she/it + s in present).
动词与故事时间或主语不一致（现在时he/she/it要加s）。		 Match every verb to the time. Past story = past tense.
让所有动词与时间一致。过去的故事用过去时。		 ✗ Yesterday, Mina walk home. ✓ walked
✗ Mina carry a bag every day. ✓ carries

B. Literary Devices · 修辞手法

Device 手法What it does 作用Example 例子
Simile 明喻Compares with like or as.
用like或as比较。		The baby slept like a log.
Metaphor 暗喻Says one thing is another (no like/as).
直接说A就是B（没有like/as）。		The classroom was a zoo.
Personification 拟人Gives human actions to a non-human thing.
让非人的事物有人的动作。		The wind whispered at the window.
Hyperbole 夸张Extreme exaggeration.
极度夸大。		I have told you a million times!
Assonance 元音重复Repeats the same vowel SOUND in nearby words.
邻近单词重复同一个元音发音。		Go slow over the road.
Quick test · 快速判断: See like / as? → simile. Says is / was about two different things? → metaphor. A thing does a human action? → personification. Too big to be true? → hyperbole. Same vowel sound repeated? → assonance.

C. Reading Strategies · 阅读策略

Context clues

Read the sentence before and after the word. Ask: what meaning fits ALL the clues?

读目标词前后的句子，找出符合所有线索的词义。

True / False

A statement is true only if EVERY part is true in the text. Find the exact line.

只有每个部分都符合原文才是真。找到原文那一句。

Eliminate

Cross out options you know are wrong first. Then compare the rest.

先排除明显错误的选项，再比较剩下的。

A. The ACE Method · ACE 答题法

A = Answer · 回答

Answer the question in a full sentence. Turn the question into the start of your answer.

用完整句子回答问题。把问题改成答案的开头。

Question: Why was Sam worried? → Sam was worried because...

C = Cite evidence · 引用证据

Give a detail from the story. Use a starter: In the story... / The text says... / For example...

给出故事中的细节。用引导语：In the story... / The text says... / For example...

E = Explain · 解释

Say HOW the evidence proves your answer. Use: This shows that... / This means...

说明证据如何证明你的答案。用：This shows that... / This means...

The Borrowed Pencil

Question: Why did Sam leave a new pencil on Rina's desk? 山姆为什么把新铅笔放在丽娜的桌上？

Level · 水平Answer · 答案What is missing? · 缺少什么？
Weak 弱He was nice.Too short. No reason, no evidence. 太短，没有原因和证据。
Better 较好Sam left a pencil because he wanted to thank Rina.Full sentence and reason, but no evidence. 有完整句子和原因，但没有证据。

How did ___ feel / respond? 感受/反应Name the feeling + show the action that proves it. 说出感受并给出证明的动作。___ felt ... He/She showed this when ...
Predict / What if? 预测A logical result + because. 合理结果加原因。If ___ had ..., then ___ might ... because ...

Part 3: Application & Problem Solving · 应用与解决问题

Application means using plot knowledge to map a story. Problem solving means using a story's lesson to solve a real problem.
应用：用情节知识梳理故事结构。解决问题：用故事中的道理解决现实问题。

A. The Plot Map · 情节图

Part 部分Ask yourself 问自己Sentence starter 句子开头
Exposition 开端
时间、地点、主角、冲突		When? Where? Who? What is the problem?The story takes place ... The protagonist is ...
Rising Action 发展
问题变得更紧张的事件		What events make the problem bigger or more tense?First, ... Then, ...
Climax 高潮
最紧张/最关键的时刻		What is the turning point? What choice or change happens?The turning point comes when ... This changes the story because ...
Falling Action 回落
高潮之后发生的事		What happens right after the climax?After this, ...
Resolution 结局
问题如何收尾		How does the story end? How is the character changed?In the end, ...
Conflict formula · 冲突公式: [Character] wants ___, but ___ gets in the way.
【人物】想要___，但___阻碍了他/她。
Example: Mina wants to join the choir, but she is afraid to sing alone.
A strong climax has three parts · 好的高潮写三点: (1) what happens, (2) what the character chooses or realizes, (3) why it changes the story.
(1) 发生了什么，(2) 人物做出什么选择或领悟，(3) 为什么这改变了故事。

The Borrowed Pencil

PartModel answer 示范答案
SettingTime: the day of a big math test. Place: a classroom.
ProtagonistSam
ConflictSam needs a pencil for the test, but he forgot his and cannot talk to anyone.

C. The Problem-Solving Steps · 解决问题四步法

P · Problem

Name the central problem AND the two competing concerns.

说出核心问题以及两个相互冲突的考虑。

C · Choices

Give two realistic choices. Give a likely result for each.

给出两个现实的选择，并说明各自可能的结果。

D · Decision

Pick the best action. Explain WHY it is the most responsible.

选择最佳行动，解释为什么它最负责任。

S · Story link

Name one specific event from the story and explain how it supports your decision.

指出故事中一个具体事件，并解释它如何支持你的决定。

Worked example · 示范

Scenario: Ava finds a wallet in the hallway with money inside. She wants to buy a new notebook, but the wallet belongs to someone.

情景：阿娃在走廊捡到一个装有钱的钱包。她想买新笔记本，但钱包是别人的。

Weak: She should be good. (No choices, no story link.)

Strong: The central problem is that Ava is tempted to keep the money, but the owner needs it back. One choice is to keep it, but the owner would lose money and Ava might feel guilty. Another choice is to give it to a teacher, so the owner gets it back. The best action is to give it to a teacher because it is honest and fair. In 'The Borrowed Pencil,' Rina helps Sam even though she could ignore him. This shows that doing the kind and right thing helps another person in real need.
