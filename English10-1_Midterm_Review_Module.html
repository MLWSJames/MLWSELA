Part A: Reading Comprehension — 3 Steps

A部分：阅读理解——三步法

1. Read the question first
先读问题

Know what you are looking for before you read.

读文章之前，先知道要找什么。

2. Find the key word
找关键词

Look for the same word in the passage. The answer is near it.

在文章里找同一个词，答案就在它附近。

3. Cross out wrong answers
排除错误选项

Remove answers that are not in the text. Pick the best one left.

划掉文章里没有的选项，在剩下的里面选最好的。

Worked example · 示范

Question: Why does the author suggest taking dogs for walks?

Key words: walks → find 'take them for walks' in the passage → the sentence before it says dogs have a lot of energy and need to stay healthy.

关键词：walks → 在文章中找到 take them for walks → 前面说狗精力很多，需要保持健康。

Study Guide: Key Reading Strategies

学习指南：重点阅读策略

1. Summarizing · 概括

What it is · 是什么

Condensing a text into its main ideas using your own words.

用自己的话把文章压缩成主要观点。

Why it matters · 为什么重要

Helps you focus on the most important points and improves retention.

帮你抓住最重要的内容，也让你记得更牢。

How to do it · 怎么做
  • Identify the topic and main idea.
  • Pick out key supporting details.
  • Write a short version that captures the essence of the text.

找出主题和中心思想；挑出关键的支持细节；写一个简短版本，抓住文章的核心。

2. Predicting · 预测

What it is · 是什么

Making an educated guess about what will happen next.

对接下来会发生什么做有依据的猜测。

Why it matters · 为什么重要

Keeps you engaged and helps you anticipate outcomes.

让你保持专注，并能预想结果。

How to do it · 怎么做
  • Use clues from the text (setting, actions, dialogue).
  • Combine with your own experience.
  • Ask: 'What's likely to happen next?'

利用文中线索（场景、动作、对话）；结合自己的经验；问自己：接下来最可能发生什么？

3. Inferring · 推断

What it is · 是什么

Drawing conclusions based on evidence and reasoning.

根据证据和推理得出结论。

Why it matters · 为什么重要

Helps uncover deeper meaning that isn't directly stated.

帮你发现文中没有直接说出的深层含义。

How to do it · 怎么做
  • Look for hints in character behavior, tone, or setting.
  • Use logic and background knowledge.
  • Ask: 'What does this suggest?'

寻找人物行为、语气或场景中的暗示；运用逻辑和已有知识；问自己：这说明了什么？

4. Visualizing · 想象画面

What it is · 是什么

Creating mental images based on descriptive language.

根据描写性的语言在脑中形成图像。

Why it matters · 为什么重要

Makes reading more vivid and memorable.

让阅读更生动，更容易记住。

How to do it · 怎么做
  • Pay attention to sensory details.
  • Picture the scene or action in your mind.
  • Sketch or describe what you imagine.

注意感官细节；在脑中想象场景或动作；画出来或描述你想象的画面。

5. Connecting · 联系

What it is · 是什么

Relating the text to your own life, other texts, or the world.

把文章和自己的生活、其他文章或世界联系起来。

Why it matters · 为什么重要

Builds personal engagement and deeper understanding.

让你更投入，理解更深。

Types · 类型
  • Text-to-self · 文本与自己
  • Text-to-text · 文本与文本
  • Text-to-world · 文本与世界

Summary and Main Idea · Predictions · Grammar Review

概括与中心思想 · 预测 · 语法复习

Main idea and summary · 中心思想与概括

Part 部分Meaning 意思Example (dogs passage) 例子
Topic 主题Who or what the passage is about (1–3 words). 文章写的是谁或什么（1–3个词）。Dogs
Main idea 中心思想The most important thing the author says about the topic. 作者对这个主题说的最重要的一件事。Dogs are energetic pets that need care.
Supporting detail 支持细节A fact that helps prove the main idea. 帮助证明中心思想的事实。They need love, food, water, and walks.
Summary rules · 概括的规则
  • Use your own words. 用自己的话。
  • Include all key ideas. 包含所有关键信息。
  • No opinions and no personal information. 不写观点，不写个人信息。

Predicting · 预测

Clue + experience = prediction · 线索 + 经验 = 预测

Find the clues. Think about what usually happens. Then say what comes next and show the evidence.

找线索，想想通常会发生什么，然后说出接下来的事，并给出证据。

Skill 技能Ask yourself 问自己Stem 句子框架

Summarization 概括What is the main idea? Which 3 things matter most? 中心思想是什么？哪三件事最重要？The passage is about ___ . First, ___ . Then, ___ . Finally, ___ .
Inference 推断What does the text say + what do I know = what is true? 文中说了什么 + 我知道什么 = 什么是真的？The text says ___ , so I infer ___ .
Remember · 记住

An inference is not written in the text. It is a conclusion from clues. Wrong answers often use words from the text but say something the text does not support.

推断的答案不会直接写在文中，而是根据线索得出的结论。错误选项常常用了文中的词，但表达了文章不支持的意思。

Part C: Language and Grammar — Tools

C部分：语言与语法——工具

Simple sentence · 简单句

One subject + one verb = one complete idea.

一个主语 + 一个动词 = 一个完整意思。

The cat jumps on the table.

Fragment · 残句

Missing a verb (or a subject). It is not a complete sentence.

缺动词（或主语），不是完整句子。

Smiles on their faces. ✗

Question · 问句

Did + subject + base verb + rest?

Did + 主语 + 动词原形 + 其他？

Did the cat jump on the table?

Contraction · 缩写

An apostrophe takes the place of missing letters.

撇号代替省略的字母。

Where's = Where is

Subject–verb agreement · 主谓一致

One thing (he, she, it, the sun) → verb + s. The sun rises. The cat jumps.

一个人或物（he, she, it, the sun）→ 动词加 s。
