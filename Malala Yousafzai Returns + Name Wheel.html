Strategy 8 · Annotating text
Read together · PART 1 · THE RETURN
¶1Nobel Peace Prize laureate Malala Yousafzai returned to Pakistan on Thursday — for the first time since the Taliban tried to end her campaign for girls' education by shooting her, then a schoolgirl, in the head.
¶2Instead of being silenced by the 2012 attack that gravely wounded her, Malala went on to become a globally recognized advocate for children's education.
¶3“The visit was kept a secret due to security issues,” a relative said. The 20-year-old was greeted at the airport in Islamabad by government and security officials, and taken to a hotel under tight security.
¶4However, she will not travel to the Swat Valley in the north, where the shooting happened. “It was a month ago when she decided to visit Pakistan at any cost,” said a second relative, who spoke anonymously out of fear of Taliban reprisals.
Q · answer with evidence: Why was Malala's visit to Pakistan kept a secret?
LEVEL 2I think ___ because ¶__ says ___.
LEVEL 4According to ¶__, "___" — this shows ___.
Model answer + rationale

Why was Malala's visit to Pakistan kept a secret?

Answer: Because the Taliban still threatened her life — the trip was too dangerous to announce.

Evidence: ¶3 says the visit “was kept a secret due to security issues,” and she traveled “under tight security.” ¶4 adds that relatives spoke anonymously “out of fear of Taliban reprisals.”

Read together · PART 2 · THE ATTACK AND THE FIGHT
¶5As an 11-year-old, Malala started an anonymous diary describing a girl's thirst for education. Today she is a student at Oxford University in England.
¶6Once known as the Switzerland of Pakistan, Swat was under the Taliban's harsh rule between 2007 and 2009. They banned music and tried to stop female education. In 2009, Pakistan's army pushed the militants largely out of the valley — but some fighters remain, and they still threaten and kill critics.
¶7On October 9, 2012, a gunman stormed her school bus in the city of Mingora and shot her in the face at point-blank range. The Taliban said the attack was meant to punish Malala for promoting Western culture and speaking out against the militants.
¶8She was critically wounded but recovered at a hospital in Britain. Since then, she has become a worldwide symbol of female empowerment. With her father Ziauddin, she founded the Malala Fund, a charity dedicated to giving all girls access to education.
Q · answer with evidence: Why did the Taliban shoot Malala in 2012?
LEVEL 2I think ___ because ¶__ says ___.
LEVEL 4According to ¶__, "___" — this shows ___.
Model answer + rationale

Why did the Taliban shoot Malala in 2012?

Answer: To punish her for speaking out for girls' education and against the militants.

Evidence: ¶7 gives the Taliban's own reason — the attack “was meant to punish Malala for promoting Western culture and speaking out against the militants.” ¶6 shows what they opposed: female education.

Read together · PART 3 · HER VOICE
¶9“This award is not just for me,” she said when accepting the Nobel Peace Prize on December 10, 2014. “It is for those forgotten children who want education. It is for those frightened children who want peace. It is for those voiceless children who want change.”
¶10While her campaigning has taken her around the world, Malala always wanted to return to her homeland. She told TV host David Letterman she had been searching for an opportunity to travel to Pakistan. “I want my feet to touch that land and to be there,” she said.
Q · answer with evidence: What does “I want my feet to touch that land” tell us about Malala's feelings for Pakistan? (inference)
LEVEL 2I think ___ because ¶__ says ___.
LEVEL 4According to ¶__, "___" — this shows ___.
Model answer + rationale

What does “I want my feet to touch that land” tell us about Malala's feelings for Pakistan? (inference)

Answer: Sample: she deeply loves her homeland — Pakistan is still home, even after everything.

Evidence: ¶4 she decided to visit “at any cost.” ¶10 she “always wanted to return” and had been “searching for an opportunity.” Touching the land with her feet shows a physical, emotional need to be home.
