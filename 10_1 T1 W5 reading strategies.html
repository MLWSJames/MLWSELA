1. What Is an Inference? · 什么是推断？

An inference is a smart idea you make when the writer or the picture does not tell you the answer directly. You use clues and what you already know.

推断是指：作者或图片没有直接告诉你答案时，你根据线索和已有知识做出的合理想法。

CLUES (what I see or read) + WHAT I KNOW = INFERENCE 线索（我看到或读到的）＋ 我已经知道的 ＝ 推断

Fact, Inference, or Prediction? · 事实、推断，还是预测？

Type 类型Meaning 意思Example 例子
Fact
事实		What I can see or read directly.
我直接看到或读到的内容。		The woman has her head on her laptop.
Inference ✔
推断		What is happening NOW, based on clues.
根据线索，推断现在正在发生什么。		She fell asleep because she is tired.
Prediction ✘
预测		What will happen NEXT.
接下来会发生什么。		She will wake up soon.
Important: We are not predicting what comes next. We are inferring what is happening NOW.
重点：我们不是预测接下来会发生什么，而是推断现在正在发生什么。

2. Build an Inference in 3 Parts · 用三个部分写出推断

Look at Picture 1. Find the clues, then decide what is happening.

观察图片 1。先找线索，再判断发生了什么。

BEGINNING · Clue 1 · 开头：线索一

The woman has her head on her laptop.
这位女士把头靠在笔记本电脑上。

MIDDLE · Clue 2 · 中间：线索二

She is wearing work clothes and looks like she is at a desk in an office.
她穿着工作服，看起来在办公室的桌子前。

END · Inference · 结尾：推断

She fell asleep and is overworked and tired.
她睡着了，因为工作太多，很累。
Because [Clue 1] and [Clue 2], I infer that [what is happening]. 因为［线索一］和［线索二］，我推断［正在发生的事］。

Join the parts into ONE full sentence:

Because the woman has her head on the laptop and she is wearing work clothes or looks like she is in an office, I infer that she fell asleep because she is overworked and tired.

把三个部分连成一个完整的句子（如上）。

3. Strong or Weak? · 强推断还是弱推断？

Weak ✘ 弱Strong ✔ 强Why? 原因
I infer she is tired.Because her head is on the laptop and she is in work clothes, I infer she is tired.The strong one gives evidence.
强推断要给出证据。
Because the man is lying down, I infer he is sleeping.Because the man is on the floor with papers around him and there is a caution sign, I infer he slipped on a wet floor.Use all the important clues, not only one.
要用到所有重要线索，不要只用一个。
The students will go home.Because they throw caps and hold diplomas, I infer they have just graduated.Do not predict. Say what is happening now.
不要预测，要说现在发生了什么。
Checklist 检查清单: ① Did I use at least 2 clues? ② Did I write 'Because… I infer that…'? ③ Am I telling what is happening NOW?
① 我用了至少两个线索吗？② 我写了 'Because… I infer that…' 吗？③ 我说的是现在发生的事吗？

1. What Is a Summary? · 什么是概括？

A summary tells only the most important points of a text, in fewer words, using your own words.

概括是用更少的词，并且用自己的话，只说出文章最重要的要点。

SUMMARY = MAIN POINTS + OWN WORDS + FEW WORDS 概括 ＝ 主要要点 ＋ 自己的话 ＋ 字数少

2. Five Steps · 五个步骤

StepWhat to do · 怎么做
1. Read 读Read the whole text and the instructions. Note how many points you need (for example: SIX).
读完全文和要求。注意需要几个要点（例如：六个）。
2. Find 找Find the main idea of each paragraph. Underline key words.
找出每段的主要观点，给关键词画线。
3. Cut 删Cross out examples, numbers, and extra details.
划掉例子、数字和多余的细节。
4. Write 写Write one fact per line, numbered 1 to 6, in your own words, with good grammar.
每行写一个要点，编号 1 到 6，用自己的话，语法正确。
5. Count 数Count your words. Write the total at the end, for example: (Total: 45 words).
数字数，并在结尾写出总数，例如：(Total: 45 words)。

3. Worked Example: How to Be Safe · 示例：如何保证安全

Instructions 要求: Summarise SIX points that children can follow to keep themselves safe. One fact per line. Do not use more than 70 words. Show your word count. Use your own words.

概括孩子保证自身安全的六个要点。每行一个要点，不超过 70 个词，写出字数，用自己的话。

Make sure that one of your parents knows at all times where you are. Don't go off somewhere without telling someone where you are. Make sure also that your parents know when you will be returning. Your parents also need to know where you are and with whom.
Text says 原文Key words 关键词My own words 我的表达
Make sure that one of your parents knows at all times where you are…parents · know · where · goingYour parents should always be aware of where you are going.

Changing words: copy vs own words · 改写：抄原文还是用自己的话

Original 原文Copy ✘ 抄写Own words ✔ 自己的话
Never take a gift from a stranger.Never take a gift from a stranger.Don't take things from people you don't know.
Memorise a telephone number that you could use in an emergency.Memorise a telephone number you could use in an emergency.Remember emergency contact phone numbers.
Make sure that your parents meet the parents of your friends.Make sure your parents meet your friends' parents.Parents should know the parents of friends.

2. Parents should know the parents of friends.

4. How to Count Words · 如何数字数

Rule 规则Example 例子
Count every word, including small words (a, the, to, of).
每个词都要数，包括小词。		Learn how to defend yourself. = 5 words
A short form with an apostrophe is ONE word.
带撇号的缩写算一个词。		Don't = 1 word
Do NOT count the list numbers (1, 2, 3…).
不要数列表编号。		'4. Remember emergency contact phone numbers.' = 5 words
Common mistakes 常见错误: copying whole sentences · keeping examples and numbers · writing more than the word limit · forgetting to write the total.
常见错误：抄整句、保留例子和数字、超过字数限制、忘记写总字数。

1. What Are Context Clues? · 什么是语境线索？

Context clues are hints in the words around a new word. They help you work out the meaning without a dictionary.

语境线索是新单词周围文字里的提示。它们能帮你不用词典就推断出词义。

A clue can be in the same sentence, in the sentence before, or in the sentence after.

线索可能在同一个句子里，也可能在前一句或后一句。

NEW WORD + CLUES AROUND IT = MEANING 生词 ＋ 周围的线索 ＝ 词义
Link to Inferences · 与推断的联系: Finding a word's meaning from clues is an inference. You use the same skill: clues + what you know.
用线索找词义就是一种推断。技能相同：线索 ＋ 已有知识。

2. Five Types of Clues · 五种线索

Type 类型How it helps 作用Signal words 信号词Example 例子
Synonym
同义词		Another word or phrase with the same meaning is nearby.
附近有意思相同的词或短语。		or · that is · in other words · commas , dashes —The hikers were exhausted, or very tired, after the climb.
Antonym
反义词		An opposite word shows the meaning.
意思相反的词帮助你理解词义。		but · unlike · however · while · instead ofUnlike his talkative sister, Wei hardly spoke in class.
Example
举例		Examples show what the word includes.
例子说明这个词包括什么。		such as · for example · like · includingMany citrus fruits, such as lemons and oranges, taste sour.
Explanation
解释说明		The sentence around the word explains it or shows the result.
周围的句子解释这个词或说明结果。		because · so · this means · after the word or before itThe road was treacherous. It was icy, and three cars had already slid off.
Word part
词的组成部分		A prefix, root, or suffix gives a hint.
前缀、词根或后缀给出提示。		re- · un- · pre- · -less · -ablePlease rewrite your essay. (re- = again)

Part 部分Meaning 意思Example 例子

Context clues are hints that an author gives to help define a difficult or unusual word.

语境线索是作者给出的提示，帮助你理解难词或不常见的词。

A clue may appear within the same sentence as the word to which it refers, or it may come before or after the sentence.

线索可能和这个词在同一个句子里，也可能在这个句子之前或之后。

Types of Clues · 线索类型

S

3. Four Steps to Find the Meaning · 找词义的四个步骤

Step 1 · Find 找

Circle the new word.
圈出生词。

Step 2 · Read 读

Read the sentence before and the sentence after.
读前一句和后一句。

Step 3 · Name 判断

Which clue type is it? Look for signal words.
是哪种线索？找信号词。

Step 4 · Test 检验

Put your guess in the sentence. Does it make sense?
把你的猜测放进句子里，通顺吗？

Worked Example · 示例

The village was deserted. No people walked in the streets, and every shop was closed.
Step 步骤My thinking 我的思考
1. FindNew word: deserted
2. ReadThe next sentence says: 'No people walked in the streets, and every shop was closed.'
3. NameThis is an explanation clue. The sentence after the word explains it.
4. Test'The village was empty, with no people.' It makes sense. ✔

4. Explain How You Know · 解释你是怎么知道的

I infer that [word] means [meaning] because the clue '[clue words]' is a [type] clue. 我推断［单词］的意思是［词义］，因为线索 '［线索］' 是一个［类型］线索。
Weak ✘ 弱Strong ✔ 强
Deserted means empty. I just know.I infer that deserted means empty because the clue 'No people walked in the streets, and every shop was closed' is an explanation clue.
Be careful 小心: Always do the test step. If your guess does not fit the whole sentence, look for another clue. Use a dictionary to check when you can.
一定要做检验这一步。如果你的猜测不适合整个句子，就再找别的线索。可以时用词典核对。

1. What Is a Prediction? · 什么是预测？

A prediction is what you think will happen NEXT. You use clues from the text and what you already know.

预测是你认为接下来会发生什么。你要用文章中的线索和已有知识。

TEXT CLUES + WHAT I KNOW = WHAT WILL HAPPEN NEXT 文章线索 ＋ 我已经知道的 ＝ 接下来会发生什么
Skill 技能Time 时间Example 例子
Inference 推断What is happening NOW
现在正在发生什么		Because he has no umbrella and the sky is dark, I infer that he is worried about the rain.
Prediction 预测What will happen NEXT
接下来会发生什么		I predict that he will get wet, because the text says there is no roof at the bus stop.

2. Prediction Sentence Frame · 预测句型

I predict that [what will happen next] because the text says [evidence]. 我预测［接下来会发生的事］，因为文中说［证据］。
Look for 'cliffhanger' endings 注意悬念结尾: Many passages stop with three dots (...). The writer wants you to think about what happens next.
很多文章以三个点（……）结尾。作者想让你思考接下来会发生什么。

3. Worked Example · 示例

Dark clouds covered the sky. Sam had left his umbrella at home, and the bus stop had no roof. He looked at the clouds and sighed. The first drops of rain fell on his shoes. The bus was still ten minutes away...
Step 步骤Example 例子
1. What event is most likely next?
接下来最可能发生什么？		I predict that Sam will get very wet while he waits.
2. What is the evidence?
证据是什么？		He has no umbrella, the stop has no roof, rain is starting, and the bus is still ten minutes away.
3. Full sentence
完整句子		I predict that Sam will get very wet because the text says he left his umbrella at home, the bus stop has no roof, and the bus is ten minutes away.

4. Strong or Weak? · 强预测还是弱预测？

Weak ✘ 弱Strong ✔ 强Why? 原因
Sam will be happy.Sam will get wet, because he has no umbrella and the stop has no roof.The weak one has no evidence and does not fit the clues.
弱预测没有证据，也不符合线索。
Something will happen.The bus will probably be late or Sam will run for shelter, because the rain is starting.Be specific. Say what event.
要具体，说出是什么事件。
Sam will win a million dollars.Sam will arrive at school wet and cold.A prediction must be likely given the clues.
预测必须根据线索来说是很可能发生的。
Checklist 检查清单: ① Is my prediction about what happens NEXT? ② Is it likely, based on the text? ③ Did I say the evidence ('because the text says…')?
① 我的预测是关于接下来发生的事吗？② 根据文章它很可能发生吗？③ 我说出证据了吗（'because the text says…'）？

1. What Is a Connection? · 什么是联系？

A connection links what you see or read to something you already know. Connections help you understand and remember.

联系是把你看到或读到的内容和你已经知道的东西联系起来。联系能帮助你理解和记忆。

Three Kinds of Connections · 三种联系

1. Text-to-Self · 与自己

It reminds me of a time when I…
它让我想起我自己的一次经历……

2. Text-to-Text · 与其他作品

It reminds me of a (movie / book / song / story) because…
它让我想起一部电影/书/歌/故事，因为……

3. Text-to-World · 与现实世界

It makes me think about (an event from the past or real life) because…
它让我想到过去的事件或现实生活，因为……
Always say 'because' 一定要说 'because': A strong connection explains why it is connected. Each paragraph needs at least three sentences.
有力的联系要解释为什么有联系。每个段落至少写三句话。

2. Worked Example · 示例

Type 类型Model answer 示范答案
Text-to-Self
与自己		This image reminds me of a time when I went on a beach holiday with my family.
Text-to-Text
与其他作品		The picture reminds me of a movie called Jaws because in the movie people are enjoying the beach before they are attacked by a huge shark.
Text-to-World
与现实世界		What I just saw makes me think about real life summers in Jinshitan because during summer many people go to the beach here in Jinshitan and spend time having fun in the sun.

3. Strong or Weak? · 强联系还是弱联系？

Weak ✘ 弱Strong ✔ 强Why? 原因
I like the beach.This image reminds me of a time when I went on a beach holiday with my family.The strong one is a real memory linked to the picture.
强联系是和图片相关的真实回忆。
It reminds me of a movie.It reminds me of a movie called Jaws because people enjoy the beach before a shark attacks.Name the movie and say because.
要说出电影名，并用 because 解释。
Beaches are in the world.It makes me think about summers in Jinshitan because many people go to the beach here.Give a real place or event and explain.
给出真实的地点或事件，并解释。

1. What Is Visualizing? · 什么是想象画面？

Visualizing means making a picture in your mind while you read. Good readers use the writer's details to 'see' the scene, like a movie in your head.

想象画面是指在阅读时，在脑海里形成一幅画面。优秀的读者会利用作者给出的细节来『看见』场景，就像脑海里的电影。

DETAIL WORDS + MY SENSES + WHAT I KNOW = A PICTURE IN MY MIND 细节词 ＋ 我的感官 ＋ 我已经知道的 ＝ 脑海中的画面

2. Use All Five Senses · 使用五种感官

Sense 感官Ask yourself 问自己Example 例子
See 👀 看What colours, shapes, and sizes are there?
有什么颜色、形状和大小？		green eyes glowed in the dark
Hear 👂 听What sounds are there?
有什么声音？		the floor creaked under her boots

有什么气味？		the smell of warm bread and cinnamon
Taste 👅 尝What tastes are there?
有什么味道？		sweet, sticky mango juice
Touch ✋ 触What does it feel like?
摸起来、感觉起来怎样？		the sand was hot under my bare feet

4. Four Steps · 四个步骤

Step 1 · Read 读

Read slowly.
慢慢读。

Step 2 · Underline 画线

Underline detail words (colours, sounds, smells).
给细节词画线（颜色、声音、气味）。

Step 3 · Picture 想象

Close your eyes and make the scene.
闭上眼睛，想象场景。

Step 4 · Change 修改

Change your picture when new details appear.
出现新细节时，修改你的画面。
When I read [the sentence], I picture [what I see/hear/smell] because the text says [detail]. 当我读到［句子］时，我想象到［我看到/听到/闻到的］，因为文中写了［细节］。

1. Why Ask Questions? · 为什么要提问？

Good readers ask questions before, during, and after reading. Questions help you pay attention and find what you do not understand.

优秀的读者会在阅读前、阅读中、阅读后提问。提问帮助你集中注意力，并发现自己不懂的地方。

Question Words · 疑问词

Word 词Asks about 询问Example 例子
Whoa person · 人Who is Gloria looking for?
Whata thing or event · 事物或事件What did Max drop from his hand?
Wherea place · 地点Where did Mr. Thomas sit?
Whena time · 时间When did the storm begin?
Whya reason · 原因Why did Mr. Thomas sit with his head in his hands?
Howa way or feeling · 方式或感受How did Tony feel when he found the door locked?

Two Kinds of Questions · 两种问题

Right There · 答案就在文中

The answer is written in the text. Who, What, Where, When questions are often right there.
答案直接写在文中。Who、What、Where、When 类问题常常如此。

Think and Infer · 思考并推断

The text does not say it. You use clues and what you know. Why and How questions often need this.
文中没有直接说出，需要用线索和已有知识来推断。Why 和 How 类问题常常如此。

2. Worked Example: Ask, Then Infer · 示例：先提问，再推断

Worked Example · 示例：Mr. Thomas

Mr. Thomas sat on the front porch of his house in a bathrobe and bunny slippers with his head in his hands. He was holding the daily newspaper. The sun shined brightly in the sky. His neighbours were coming out of their houses and getting in their cars. Mr. Thomas sighed. His neighbour Mr. Wu stepped out on his porch and saw Mr. Thomas. 'Did it again, huh?' asked Mr. Wu. 'Yep.' Mr. Thomas replied. Mr. Wu chuckled and said, 'Here, you can use my cell phone. I could even keep a key over at my house for you if you wanted.' Mr. Thomas thankfully took the cell phone and called his wife.
Step 步骤Example 例子
a) Ask 2 questions
提出两个问题		1. Why did Mr. Thomas sit with his head in his hands?
2. What did Mr. Thomas do again?
b) Find the clues
找线索		Clue 1: He is in a bathrobe and slippers, so it is early morning.
Clue 2: Mr. Wu asks, 'Did it again, huh?' and offers to keep a key.
c) Write the inference
写出推断		Because Mr. Thomas is in his bathrobe and slippers, it is early in the morning, and Mr. Wu asks if he did it again, I infer that Mr. Thomas left his house without his key and accidentally locked himself out.
