Strategy 6 · Frequent checks
Model answer

What does "reliable" mean in the sentence?

Answer: B — you can trust it.

Context clue: NASA is a famous science organization. A trusted source makes the numbers trustworthy. A and C describe style, not trust.

Group 2 · ESSAY STRUCTURE WORDS

Words for building a response

topic sentencefirst sentence; tells the main idea of a paragraph
thesisthe main argument of the whole essay
transitiona word that connects ideas (however, also, first)
explanationyour own words saying what the evidence means
conclusionthe ending; repeats the main idea in new words
supportdetails that make your idea stronger
Strategy 4 · Vocabulary enhancement
Quick check · hold up A, B or C

Which sentence is a THESIS?

A"First, the infographic shows a big green foot."
B"Both infographics inform readers, but only one persuades us to act."
C"In conclusion, I read two texts."
Strategy 6 · Frequent checks
Model answer

Which sentence is a thesis?

Answer: B.

A thesis gives your main ARGUMENT — it compares both texts and takes a position. A is only a detail (evidence), and C is a conclusion sentence.

Group 3 · CITATION WORDS

Words for using other people's ideas

quotationthe writer's exact words, in "…" marks
citationnaming where the words or facts came from
paraphrasesaying the same idea in your own words
plagiarismcopying without saying the source — not OK
attributeto say WHO said or wrote it
summarizegive only the main ideas, much shorter
Strategy 4 · Vocabulary enhancement
Quick check · hold up A, B or C

You write the infographic's idea in your OWN words and name the source. This is…

Aa paraphrase
Ba quotation
Cplagiarism
Strategy 6 · Frequent checks
Model answer

Your own words + naming the source = ?

Answer: A — a paraphrase.

Own words = paraphrase. Exact words in "…" = quotation. Own words WITHOUT the source = plagiarism — that is the trap.

Model answer

Answer: A — to shock the reader.

Design is a choice. Giant size + bright colour = emphasis. The designer wants you to feel the size of the problem before you read anything else.
