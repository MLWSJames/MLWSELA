Part 1 · Rules for EVERY Essay 第一部分：每篇文章都要遵守的规则

Before you learn the paragraphs, learn the four rules. They apply to the introduction, the body, and the conclusion.

在学习各个段落之前，先记住四条规则。它们适用于引言、主体和结论。

🚫 1. No first person
不用第一人称

Do not write: I, me, my, my opinion, my ideas.

不要写：I, me, my, my opinion, my ideas。

🚫 2. No contractions
不用缩写

Write it out: did not, would not, is not.

要写完整：did not, would not, is not。

🎩 3. Formal language
使用正式语言

Choose serious, precise words. Avoid slang and casual words.

选择严肃、准确的词。避免俚语和口语。

🔗 4. Transition words
使用过渡词

Start EACH body paragraph and the concluding paragraph with a transition.

每个主体段和结论段都要用过渡词开头。

Do NOT write ❌ 不要这样写Write this ✅ 要这样写
In my opinion, homework is too long.Homework is too long for most students.
Teachers don't understand students.Teachers do not always understand students.
Kids get really stressed out.Children experience serious stress.

Part 2 · Paragraph 1: The Introduction 第二部分：第一段——引言

An introduction has three parts, always in this order. It also earns 3 points for linking to your prompt.

引言有三个部分，顺序固定。与题目建立联系还可以得 3 分。

1️⃣ HOOK 钩子句

The FIRST sentence. Average length: 2–3 sentences. It shows readers the essay is worth reading. NOT A QUESTION.

第一句话。通常 2–3 句。让读者觉得文章值得读。不能是问句。

2️⃣ LEAD-IN STATEMENT 引导句

Gives the reader an idea of what your essay is about.

让读者大概知道文章要讲什么。

3️⃣ THESIS 论点句

Your argument or point of view: clear, concise, compelling. Usually the LAST sentence of the introduction. It links to your prompt.

你的论点或观点：清楚、简洁、有说服力。通常是引言的最后一句。要联系题目。

Worked example 范例

Prompt: Should schools start later in the morning? 题目：学校是否应该晚一点开始上课？

HookLead-inThesis
Across the world, millions of teenagers drag themselves out of bed before sunrise to reach their first class. Sleep plays a major role in how well students think, remember, and behave at school. Schools should start later in the morning because extra sleep improves learning, protects health, and reduces stress.

中文大意：钩子句——全世界有数百万青少年在日出前起床上学。引导句——睡眠影响学生的思考、记忆和行为。论点句——学校应该晚点上课，因为更多睡眠能提高学习效果、保护健康、减轻压力。

Thesis formula · 论点句公式: Topic + Position + 2–3 reasons.
Schools should start later (topic + position) because extra sleep improves learning, protects health, and reduces stress (3 reasons).

Mistake ❌ 错误Why it is wrong 原因Fix ✅ 改正

Part 1 · Paragraphs 2, 3, 4: Body Paragraphs 第一部分：第2、3、4段——主体段

Each body paragraph proves ONE part of your thesis. Use this formula every time:

每个主体段证明论点的一个部分。每次都使用这个公式：

Transition + Point. Reporting phrase + Evidence + MLA citation. Explanation. Transition + Concluding sentence.
过渡词 + 观点句。引述短语 + 证据 + MLA 引用。解释。过渡词 + 总结句。

🔗 T + P · Transition + Point 过渡词 + 观点句

Start with a transition word. Then state the point of the paragraph. The point links to your thesis.

先用过渡词，再写本段观点。观点要联系引言中的论点句。

📖 E · Evidence 证据

Introduce it with a reporting phrase: In the text 'Title,' the author explains that... Then quote, paraphrase, or summarize. Add an MLA citation.

先用引述短语引出，再引用、改写或概括文本内容，最后加 MLA 引用。

💡 E · Explanation 解释

Explain WHY the evidence supports your prompt. Use your OWN words to show understanding.

解释证据为什么支持你的观点。用你自己的话表达理解。

🔚 C · Concluding sentence 总结句

Start with a transition. Restate the point in other words and link the evidence back to it.

用过渡词开头。换一种说法重述观点，把证据和观点联系起来。

Part 3 · Worked Example 第三部分：范例

Prompt: How does the author show the value of persistence? 题目：作者如何表现坚持的价值？

Text: 'The Broken Kite' by Anna Reyes

Tomas worked for three weeks on his kite. On the first windy day, a gust tore the paper in half. He wanted to throw it away. His grandfather only smiled and handed him new paper and glue. 'A kite does not fly because it is perfect,' the old man said. 'It flies because someone keeps trying.' Tomas rebuilt it twice more before it finally rose above the rooftops.
TransitionPointReporting phrase + EvidenceMLA citationExplanationConcluding sentence
Firstly, the author demonstrates that persistence can turn failure into success. In the text 'The Broken Kite,' Anna Reyes explains that Tomas rebuilt his kite two more times before it finally rose above the rooftops (Reyes 1). This detail proves that the first failure was not the end of the story; each new attempt brought Tomas closer to a kite that could fly. Therefore, persistence changes failure into a step toward success.

中文大意：首先，作者表明坚持可以把失败变成成功。在《破风筝》中，Anna Reyes 说明 Tomas 又重做了两次风筝，最后它飞过了屋顶（Reyes 1）。这个细节证明第一次失败不是故事的结尾；每一次新尝试都让他更接近能飞的风筝。因此，坚持把失败变成通向成功的一步。

Notice · 注意: The explanation does NOT copy the evidence. It adds new thinking in the writer's own words. There is no first person and no contraction.
解释部分没有照抄证据，而是用自己的话加入新的思考。没有第一人称，也没有缩写。

Part 1 · Paragraph 5: The Conclusion 第一部分：第5段——结论

The conclusion has three jobs. It starts with a transition word.

结论段有三项任务，并且要用过渡词开头。

1️⃣ Restate the thesis 重述论点

Say your thesis again using DIFFERENT words.

用不同的词再说一次论点。

2️⃣ Final thoughts 最后的思考

Give a closing idea about the question that was asked. Do NOT add new evidence.

对题目给出收尾的想法。不要加入新证据。

3️⃣ Answer the prompt 回答题目

Make sure the reader can clearly see your answer to the prompt.

确保读者能清楚看到你对题目的回答。

Worked example 范例

Original thesis: Schools should start later in the morning because extra sleep improves learning, protects health, and reduces stress.
TransitionRestated thesisFinal thoughts
In conclusion, delaying the first bell would clearly benefit students, since additional rest strengthens learning, supports physical and mental health, and lowers pressure. Ultimately, giving young people more sleep is an investment in better learning for the whole community.

中文大意：总之，推迟第一节课的铃声显然对学生有益，因为更多休息能增强学习、支持身心健康、减轻压力。归根结底，让年轻人多睡觉，是对整个社区更好学习的投资。

Check · 检查: The restatement keeps the same idea but changes the words (start later → delaying the first bell; extra sleep → additional rest).
重述保留了相同的意思，但换了词语（start later → delaying the first bell；extra sleep → additional rest）。
