Part 1: Figurative Language · 比喻性语言

Figurative language compares or describes things in ways that are not literally true. Poets use it to create feeling and meaning.

比喻性语言用不完全真实的方式来比较或描述事物。诗人用它来营造情感和意义。

Simile 明喻 — uses "like" or "as"

Directly compares two different things using the words "like" or "as."

用"像"或"如"直接比较两种不同的事物。

Example: Her smile was as bright as the sun.
Metaphor 隐喻 — no "like" or "as"

States that one thing IS another thing, without using "like" or "as." It is a stronger, more direct comparison than a simile.

直接说某事物"就是"另一事物，不使用"像"或"如"，比明喻更直接更强烈。

Example: Her heart is a locked door, rusted shut by years of silence.
How to tell them apart · 如何区分它们

Ask yourself: does the sentence use the word "like" or "as"? If yes, it is a simile. If the sentence just says something IS something else, it is a metaphor.

问自己：句子里有没有"像"或"如"？有，就是明喻；如果直接说某物"是"另一物，就是隐喻。

Personification 拟人

Gives human actions, feelings, or qualities to something that is not human — an object, animal, or idea.

把人的动作、情感或特征赋予非人类的事物——物体、动物或概念。

Example: The old house groaned as the storm arrived.
Hyperbole 夸张

An extreme exaggeration used for emphasis or humor. It is never meant to be taken literally.

用于强调或幽默的极端夸张说法，不能按字面理解。

Example: I've asked you a thousand times to pick up your shoes!
Symbolism 象征

An object, person, animal, or color that stands for a deeper idea beyond its literal meaning.

一个物体、人物、动物或颜色代表其字面意义之外的更深层含义。

Example: A dove landing gently often symbolizes peace.

Part 2: Sound Devices · 声音手法

Sound devices are about how a poem sounds when read aloud, not just what it means.

声音手法关注诗歌朗读时的声音效果，而不仅仅是意义。

Alliteration 头韵

The repetition of the same consonant sound at the beginning of words that are close together.

相邻词语开头重复出现相同的辅音。

Example: Silver stars scattered across a black blanket.
Assonance 元音韵

The repetition of vowel sounds inside nearby words (not necessarily at the start).

相邻词语内部元音重复出现（不一定在开头）。

Example: The rain in Spain falls mainly on the plain.
Onomatopoeia 拟声词

A word that imitates the actual sound it describes.

模仿其所描述的真实声音的词语。

Example: The bacon sizzled and popped in the pan.
Rhyme Scheme 韵律格式

The pattern of end-rhymes in a poem, labeled with letters. Lines that rhyme get the same letter.

诗歌中行末押韵的模式，用字母标记，押韵的行用同一字母表示。

Example: AABB means lines 1-2 rhyme, and lines 3-4 rhyme with each other.
Meter 格律

The rhythmic pattern of stressed and unstressed syllables in a line.

诗行中重读与非重读音节形成的节奏模式。

Example: Shakespeare's sonnets often use iambic pentameter — ten syllables per line.
Worked Example · 示范练习
The bacon sizzled, popped, and hissed, Silver smoke curled from the pan. The kitchen sang its morning song before the family day began.

Sound devices found: "sizzled, popped, hissed" = onomatopoeia; "Silver smoke" = alliteration; "kitchen sang" = personification (bonus figurative device).

发现的声音手法："sizzled, popped, hissed"是拟声词；"Silver smoke"是头韵；"kitchen sang"是拟人（额外的比喻手法）。

Part 1: Structured Poetic Forms · 结构化诗歌体裁

Some poems follow strict rules about the number of lines, the rhyme scheme, or the syllable count. These are called fixed forms.

有些诗歌在行数、韵律或音节数上遵循严格规则，这些被称为固定体裁。

Sonnet 十四行诗 (14 lines)

Shakespearean (English) Sonnet: 14 lines, three quatrains (4-line groups) plus a final rhyming couplet, rhyme scheme ABAB CDCD EFEF GG. Often about love or beauty.

莎士比亚（英式）十四行诗：14行，三个四行诗节加一个押韵对句，韵律为ABAB CDCD EFEF GG，常写爱情或美。

Petrarchan (Italian) Sonnet: 14 lines divided into an 8-line octave and a 6-line sestet, with a "turn" in thought between them.

彼特拉克（意大利）十四行诗：14行，分为8行的八行组和6行的六行组，两部分之间有思路的"转折"。

Haiku 俳句 (3 lines, 5-7-5)

A Japanese form with three lines and a 5-7-5 syllable pattern. Traditionally focuses on nature and a single moment in time.

日式诗体，三行，音节为5-7-5，传统上聚焦自然和某一瞬间。

Example: Autumn leaves falling (5) / softly onto quiet ground (7) / winter waits close by (5)
Limerick 打油诗 (5 lines, AABBA)

A humorous five-line poem. Lines 1, 2, and 5 rhyme with each other and are longer; lines 3 and 4 rhyme with each other and are shorter.

幽默的五行诗，第1、2、5行押韵且较长，第3、4行押韵且较短。

Ballad 民谣叙事诗

A poem that tells a story, often meant to be sung, usually in four-line stanzas with an ABCB rhyme scheme.

讲述故事的诗歌，常配乐演唱，通常为四行诗节，韵律为ABCB。

Ode 颂歌

A formal poem written to praise or celebrate a person, object, or idea.

用于赞美或歌颂某人、某物或某种理念的正式诗歌。

Elegy 挽歌

A poem written to mourn someone's death, expressing grief, loss, and often reflection on life.

悼念逝者的诗歌，表达悲伤、失落，常常也反思人生。

Villanelle 维拉内拉诗

A 19-line poem with two repeating refrain lines and a strict rhyme pattern across five tercets and a final quatrain.

19行诗，含两句反复出现的叠句，五个三行诗节加一个四行诗节，韵律严格。

Acrostic 藏头诗

A poem in which the first letter of each line spells out a word or message when read top to bottom.

每行第一个字母从上到下连起来能拼出一个词或一句话的诗歌。

Part 2: Open / Free Forms · 自由体裁

Not every poem follows strict rules. Some forms break away from fixed patterns on purpose.

不是每首诗都遵循严格规则，有些体裁故意打破固定模式。

Free Verse 自由诗

Poetry with no fixed rhyme scheme or meter. Line length and rhythm follow the natural flow of thought and speech.

没有固定韵律或格律的诗歌，行的长度与节奏跟随思路和语言的自然流动。

Blank Verse 无韵诗

Poetry written in a steady meter (usually iambic pentameter) but without rhyme.

采用稳定格律（通常是抑扬格五音步）但不押韵的诗歌。

Concrete / Shape Poetry 图形诗

Poetry arranged on the page so its shape visually represents its subject.

诗行在页面上排列成与主题相关的图形，通过视觉形状表达意义。

Lyric vs. Narrative Poetry · 抒情诗与叙事诗

Lyric poetry expresses a speaker's personal thoughts and feelings; it usually does not tell a full story. Narrative poetry tells a story with characters and events, like a ballad or an epic.

抒情诗表达说话者个人的思想和情感，通常不完整讲述一个故事；叙事诗则讲述带有人物和事件的故事，例如民谣或史诗。

Part 1: Speaker, Tone, and Mood · 说话者、语气与氛围

Speaker 说话者

The voice speaking in the poem. The speaker is NOT always the poet — it can be an imagined character, an animal, or even an object.

诗中说话的声音。说话者不总是诗人本人，可能是虚构的角色、动物，甚至是物体。

Tone 语气

The speaker's or poet's attitude toward the subject — for example, hopeful, angry, sarcastic, or nostalgic.

说话者或诗人对主题的态度——例如充满希望、愤怒、讽刺或怀旧。

Mood 氛围

The feeling or atmosphere the poem creates in the reader. Tone comes from the writer; mood is what the reader experiences.

诗歌在读者心中营造的感觉或氛围。语气来自作者，而氛围是读者所感受到的。

Tone vs. Mood in one sentence · 一句话区分语气与氛围

Tone = the writer's attitude. Mood = the reader's feeling.

语气＝作者的态度；氛围＝读者的感受。

Part 2: Theme · 主题

The theme is the central message or big idea of a poem — what it says about life, people, or the world. A theme is usually a full idea, not just a single word like "love."

主题是诗歌的核心信息或大观点——它对人生、人性或世界所表达的看法。主题通常是一个完整的观点，而不仅仅是一个单词，比如"爱"。

Finding the theme · 寻找主题的方法

Ask: What is this poem really about, beneath the surface? What is it trying to teach the reader about life?

问自己：这首诗表面之下真正在讲什么？它想教会读者关于人生的什么道理？

Part 3: Structure and Line Breaks · 结构与分行

Stanza 诗节

A group of lines forming one unit, similar to a paragraph in prose.

构成一个单元的若干诗行，类似于散文中的段落。

Enjambment 跨行

When a sentence or thought continues onto the next line without a pause or punctuation. It can create momentum, suspense, or mirror hesitant emotion.

句子或思路不停顿地延续到下一诗行，可以制造动感、悬念，或表现犹豫不决的情绪。

Example: I want to tell you / everything I feel
End-Stopped Line 完整句行

A line that ends with a natural pause or punctuation, such as a period or comma — the opposite of enjambment.

诗行结尾有自然停顿或标点符号，如句号或逗号——与跨行相反。

Poets choose line breaks carefully. Short, choppy lines can feel urgent or uncertain; long, flowing lines can feel calm or steady.

诗人精心选择分行方式。短促的诗行让人感到急迫或不安；流畅悠长的诗行则让人感到平静或稳定。

Part 4: A Close-Reading Method · 精读方法

Use this simple step-by-step method to analyze any poem, adapted from the well-known TP-CASTT strategy.

使用以下简单的分步方法来赏析任何诗歌，改编自著名的TP-CASTT策略。

Step 步骤What to Do 做什么
1. Title 标题Predict what the poem might be about before reading closely.
2. Paraphrase 释义Restate what happens in the poem in your own simple words.
3. Connotation 内涵Notice figurative language, imagery, and symbols — what deeper meanings do they suggest?
4. Attitude / Tone 态度/语气Identify the speaker's attitude toward the subject.
5. Shifts 转折Look for any change in tone, time, or perspective within the poem.
6. Title (again) 再看标题Revisit the title — does it mean something new now?
7. Theme 主题State the poem's central message in one full sentence.
Worked Example · 示范练习
The river does not ask permission to carry the fallen leaves away. It simply moves, patient and certain, toward the sea.

Paraphrase: A river carries leaves without asking, moving steadily toward the ocean. Connotation: the river may symbolize time or life moving forward. Tone: calm, accepting. Theme: life moves forward steadily, whether or not we are ready for it.

释义：河流不经允许带走落叶，稳定地流向大海。内涵：河流可能象征时间或人生的前进。语气：平静、坦然。主题：无论我们是否准备好，人生都会稳步向前。
