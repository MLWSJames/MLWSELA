Why Punctuation Matters

Punctuation tells the reader how a sentence should sound and what kind of sentence it is. Without it, writing is confusing.

标点符号告诉读者一句话应该怎样朗读，以及它是什么类型的句子。没有标点符号，写作会让人感到困惑。

Three Ways to End a Sentence

MarkUsed forExample
Period ( . )
句号		A statement — telling somethingI live in Dalian.
Question Mark ( ? )
问号		A question — asking somethingWhere do you live?
Exclamation Mark ( ! )
感叹号		Strong feeling — surprise, excitement, warningWatch out!
Tip: Read your sentence out loud. If your voice rises at the end, it probably needs a question mark. If you feel strong emotion, try an exclamation mark. Otherwise, use a period. 提示：把句子读出来。如果声音在结尾上扬，可能需要问号。如果表达强烈情感，可以用感叹号。否则，用句号。

Quotation Marks & Dialogue

Quotation marks ( " " ) surround the exact words a person says. Other punctuation, like a comma or a period, usually goes inside the closing quotation mark.

引号（" "）围住一个人说出的原话。其他标点符号，比如逗号或句号，通常放在闭合引号的内部。

Correct: She said, "I am ready."
Correct: "Are you coming?" asked Mei.
Incorrect: She said I am ready.

Capitalization Rules

RuleExample
Capitalize the first word of every sentence
句子的第一个词要大写		The students study every night.
Capitalize proper nouns (specific names, places)
专有名词（具体名字、地名）要大写		I live in Dalian, China.
Capitalize the first word inside a quotation
引号内句子的第一个词要大写		She said, "Good job today."

What Makes a Sentence Complete?

A complete sentence has a subject (who or what) and a verb (the action), and it expresses a full idea. A sentence fragment is missing one of these things.

一个完整的句子有主语（谁或什么）和谓语（动作），并且表达完整的意思。句子片段缺少其中一个部分。

Complete Sentence · 完整句Sentence Fragment · 句子片段
The students study every night.Because it was raining.
She forgot her homework.Running to the bus stop.

Subject-Verb Agreement

The subject and verb of a sentence must match in number: a singular subject uses a singular verb, and a plural subject uses a plural verb form.

句子的主语和谓语在数上必须一致：单数主语用单数动词形式，复数主语用复数动词形式。

Subject TypeVerb FormExample
Singular (she, he, it, one person)
单数		verb + -sShe studies every day.
Plural (they, we, more than one)
复数		base verb, no -sThey study every day.

Joining Sentences: Conjunctions

A conjunction connects two words, phrases, or sentences. The seven coordinating conjunctions are easy to remember with the word FANBOYS.

连词用来连接两个词、短语或句子。七个并列连词可以用单词FANBOYS来记忆。

ConjunctionShowsExample
andadditionI like tea and coffee.
butcontrastI like tea, but I prefer coffee.
orchoiceDo you want tea or coffee?
soresultIt was raining, so we stayed inside.

Commas: Two Jobs You Need to Know

Job 1 — Separating items in a list: I bought apples, bananas, and oranges.
Job 2 — Before a conjunction joining two complete sentences: It was raining, so we stayed inside.
Tip: Before you add a comma with a conjunction, check that both halves of the sentence could stand alone. If they can, use a comma before the conjunction. 提示：在连词前加逗号之前，检查句子的两部分是否都能独立成句。如果可以，就在连词前加逗号。

Compound Sentences

A compound sentence joins two complete sentences with a comma and a conjunction, like this: [complete sentence], [conjunction] [complete sentence].

并列句用逗号和连词连接两个完整的句子，格式为：[完整句]，[连词] [完整句]。

Example: He studied hard, and he passed the test.
