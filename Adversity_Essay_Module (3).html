PartDefinition中文

Lead-inA sentence or two that introduces the topic.一两句话引出主题。
Thesis StatementA clear statement that directly answers the prompt and outlines key points.直接回答题目并列出要点的清晰陈述。

3. The 3-point thesis and parallelism 三点式论点与平行结构

A strong thesis does two jobs: it answers the prompt and it lists your 3 body-paragraph points in order. The 3 points must be parallel: they use the same grammar form.
好的论点句做两件事：回答题目，并按顺序列出三个主体段落的要点。三个要点必须平行，即语法形式相同。

Formula 公式: Topic + verb + object by (-ing point 1), (-ing point 2), and (-ing point 3).
✅ Parallel (all -ing): Technology helps students by saving time, connecting teachers with families, and improving access to books.
✅ Parallel (all nouns): Sports teach teens discipline, teamwork, and confidence.
❌ Not parallel: Technology helps students by saving time, to connect with teachers, and improvement of grades.
❌ Only 2 points / opinion: I think technology is good because it saves time and helps learning.
Your points must match your body paragraphs in the same order. Body 1 = point 1, Body 2 = point 2, Body 3 = point 3.
论点句中要点的顺序必须与主体段落顺序一致：主体段 1 = 要点 1，主体段 2 = 要点 2，主体段 3 = 要点 3。

Poem, 2 linesUse / where the line breaks. Cite both lines."Each trial sharpens, each wound heals, / Through every ache, a truth reveals" ("Through the Storm" lines 5-6).
  • The period goes AFTER the citation. ✅ ...adapt" (Carter). ❌ ...adapt." (Carter) 句号放在引用括号后面。
  • Copy quotations exactly, word for word. Use [brackets] for a change and ... for words you skip. 引文必须逐字照抄；改动用 [ ]，省略用 ...
  • Introduce every quotation with your own words first (a signal phrase). Never start a sentence with a floating quote. 每条引文前要先用自己的话引入，不要直接用引文开头。
  • No year and no first name for the article. Use par. (not "p.") if you add a paragraph number. The number must match the paragraph where your quotation really is. 文章引用不要写年份、名字或 p.。
  • This module's checker reads your quotation and looks for it in the real text. If your words or line numbers are wrong, it will tell you. 检查器会在原文中查找你的引文，措辞或行号不对都会提示。

5. Synthesized explanation vs. summary 综合解释 vs. 简单复述

A summary repeats what the evidence says. A synthesized explanation shows how the pieces of evidence connect and why together they prove your point.
复述只是重复证据的内容；综合解释说明几条证据如何联系在一起，以及它们为什么共同证明你的观点。

Point: Teamwork improves performance.
Evidence 1: A soccer team that practices together passes more accurately.
Evidence 2: A choir that rehearses together stays in tune.
❌ Summary: Soccer teams practice. Choirs rehearse too.
✅ Synthesis: Both examples show that shared practice builds trust, and this trust proves that teamwork improves group performance.

Useful linking words: both, together, similarly, likewise, also, whereas, just as, in the same way. No quotations in the synthesis: use your own words.

Step 3: Plan your 3 points 第三步：计划三个要点

Choose 3 different ideas that answer the prompt. Write each as a short, parallel phrase (1 to 4 words). These become your thesis AND your 3 body paragraphs.
选择三个不同的想法来回答题目。每个写成简短、平行的短语（1–4 个词）。它们将成为论点句，也是三个主体段落。
