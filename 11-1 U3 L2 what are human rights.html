Strategy 4 · Vocabulary enhancement
Reading · Part 1 of 3 — What is a human right?

Human rights are freedoms. All humans are entitled to them — we are born with them.

It does not matter what your race, religion, gender, or age is. They belong to everyone.

Human rights let everyone live a life without fear.

Reading · Part 2 of 3 — Where do they come from?

One promise, signed by the world

1948After World War II, world leaders met in Paris.
UDHRThey wrote the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
30It lists 30 basic rights for every person on Earth.

Almost every country in the world agreed to this promise.

Reading · Part 3 of 3 — Universal rights (1)

Rights that protect your life and safety

The right to lifeNo one may take your life.
Freedom from tortureNo one may hurt you to punish or scare you.
Freedom from slaveryNo one may own another person.
The right to a fair trialInnocent until proven guilty, in public, with a defense.

Strategy 6 · Frequent checks
Model answer

Which one is NOT a universal human right yet?

Answer: C — the right to carry weapons.

A fair trial and clean water are in the UDHR. Weapons are still in debate — countries disagree about them.
